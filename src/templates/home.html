The back yard

Two creeks, a hot tub, and nobody else

The chalet sits on two-thirds of an acre with two creeks running through the grounds, and the Inyo National Forest begins at the back fence. The outdoor hydrotherapy tub seats six, runs all year, and looks straight up at Carson Peak. In a good snow year, skiers with a guide come down off June Mountain and finish their day in it.

Sleeps eight

Every bedroom is its own retreat

Three suites upstairs, each with its own bath. The master has a California King, its own indoor two-person jacuzzi, and a loft with a pull-out sofa. The Elsie Room has a California King and a ruby-red claw-foot tub. The River Room has a queen, and Yost Creek below the window. A pull-out futon in the Grand Room sleeps the last two.

The Grand Room

Everyone around one fire

Twenty-one feet of log ceiling over an open fireplace and an oak bar. A remodeled kitchen with a handmade copper sink, and an eleven-and-a-half-foot pine table so the whole group eats together. Nobody has to leave the fire early.
