The back yard

Yost Creek runs through the back yard

Two-thirds of an acre, two creeks, and the Inyo National Forest at the back fence. The big creek is Yost Creek, and it is a blue-line stream. On the government topo maps, a solid blue line means water that runs all year, not a gully that goes dry by August. Yost comes down cold and clear from Yost Lake, so it is there in June and still there in October.

It is the sound you hear from the outdoor hydrotherapy tub, which seats six, runs all year and looks straight up at Carson Peak. It is the sound that comes through the River Room window on a summer night. Leave the window open.

Out the back door

The trailhead is the back yard

Leave the car where it is. Hike up to Fern Lake, a cold, clear cirque under Carson Peak. Walk up Yost Creek into Yost Meadow with the kids. Climb the back side of Carson Peak and come down the scree run, sliding in long gravel steps. Little kids can do it, though it is not for everybody. In winter, walk home from Fern Creek in the snow as the light goes.

For families

Kids 12 and under ski free at June Mountain

For most families, this is the deciding number. A weekend of kids' lift tickets can cost more than the house. At June Mountain, a mile from the door, kids 12 and under ski free all season. When their legs quit, you are back by the fire in ten minutes, and the cart ride home is the best part of the day.

Sleeps eight

Every bedroom is its own retreat

Three suites upstairs, each with its own bath. The master has a California King, its own indoor two-person jacuzzi, and a loft with a pull-out sofa. The Elsie Room has a California King and a ruby-red claw-foot tub. The River Room has a queen, and Yost Creek below the window. A pull-out futon in the family room off the kitchen sleeps the last two.
