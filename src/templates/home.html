The Grand Room

Gather by the fire after the mountain

Ceilings rise 21 feet and six inches over an open fireplace and an oak bar, its stools cut from local timber. The kitchen is fully remodeled, with a handmade copper sink, and the dining table runs eleven and a half feet. There is room for everyone, and nobody has to leave the fire early.

Four seasons

One base camp for all of the Eastern Sierra

Winter

June Mountain is a mile away, and kids 12 and under ski there free. Mammoth is thirty minutes. With a guide, ski from the top of June Mountain to the hot tub.

Winter

Spring

When Tioga Road opens, the high country comes back. Spring corn snow at Tioga Pass, and the first trout of the season.

Tioga Pass

Summer

Five paddleboards in the garage, four lakes on the loop, Fern Lake above the house and Tuolumne Meadows in 45 minutes.

Summer

Fall

In October the aspens of the June Lake Loop turn gold, and the summer crowds go home.

The tub under Carson Peak

Ski tracks that end in hot water

The outdoor hydrotherapy tub sits off the back deck beneath the peak you see from every window. In winter, guests have been known to bury drinks in the snowbank around it before heading out, and to come back down the mountain on skis to find them.
