<p class="ch-intro">A hand-built log home of four thousand square feet on the June Lake Loop, rented whole to one party of up to eight. Granite in every window, a creek through the grounds, and a hot tub under Carson Peak.</p>

<h2>The Grand Room</h2>
<p>The heart of the house rises twenty-one and a half feet over an open fireplace. The oak bar has its own row of bucket stools, each cut from local timber, and a collection of Kachina dolls watches from above the mantel.</p>
<p>It is the room where the day ends and tomorrow's plans get made.</p>
</div>
</section>

<div class="ch-copy">
<h2>Kitchen and dining</h2>
<p>Completely remodeled, with a six-burner stove, a double oven and a handmade copper sink from Mexico. It is stocked down to the coffee grinder and the garlic press. Bring food, and nothing else.</p>
<p>Dinner is served at an eleven-and-a-half-foot table of local pine. The family room next door has a wood stove, and it is where the late conversations end up.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="ch-split">

<h2>The tub and the grounds</h2>
<p>The outdoor hydrotherapy tub seats six and runs all year, just off the back deck under Carson Peak. Yost Creek runs through the grounds, and the trail to Fern Lake starts a mile down-canyon from the back door.</p>
<p>Five paddleboards wait in the garage for Silver Lake and Rush Creek.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="ch-amen" aria-labelledby="in-the-house">
<h2 id="in-the-house">In the house</h2>
<div class="ch-amen-cols">
<div>
<h3>To soak</h3>
<p>Outdoor hydrotherapy tub for six, open all year. A two-person jacuzzi in the master suite. A claw-foot tub in the Elsie Room.</p>
<h3>To sleep</h3>
<p>Two California Kings and a queen in the suites, all memory foam, plus a pull-out couch in the loft and a queen futon in the family room. Organic cotton sheets and towels.</p>
</div>
<div>
<h3>To play</h3>
<p>Five stand-up paddleboards, a backgammon table by the fire, and, for a stormy afternoon, a small climbing wall in the garage.</p>
<h3>To live</h3>
<p>Wifi throughout, a high-end washer and dryer, a mud room for skis and boots, firewood waiting on arrival, and parking for three cars.</p>
</div>
