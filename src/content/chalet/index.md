## The Grand Room

{{ figure("grand-room-fireside-gathering", "Friends around the open fire in the Grand Room", "The Grand Room on a winter night.", "wide") }}

The heart of the house rises 21 feet and six inches over an open fireplace. The oak bar has its own row of bucket stools, each cut from locally sourced timber, and a collection of Kachina dolls sits above the mantel. It is the room where plans for tomorrow get made.

## Kitchen and dining

{{ figure("kitchen-copper-sink", "The remodeled kitchen with its handmade copper sink", None, "right") }}

The kitchen is completely remodeled, with all new appliances and a handmade copper sink from Mexico. It is stocked down to the coffee grinder and the garlic press, and we leave salt and olive oil. Bring food, and nothing else.

Dinner is served at an eleven-and-a-half-foot table made from local timber, long enough for everyone and the next morning's maps.

The family room next to the kitchen has a wood stove and a queen futon, and it is where the late conversations end up.

## Outside

{{ figure("chalet-lawn-lodgepole-pines", "The chalet's lawn among lodgepole pines", None, "wide") }}

Yost Creek runs through the grounds, and the trail to Fern Lake and Yost Lake starts about a mile down-canyon on foot. The outdoor hydrotherapy tub sits off the back deck under Carson Peak. The Carson Peak Inn is across the street, and the Double Eagle's Creekside Spa is a fifteen-minute walk.

## Comforts

- Outdoor hydrotherapy tub, two-person jacuzzi, and a claw-foot tub
- Wifi throughout
- High-end washer and dryer in the garage
- Organic cotton sheets and towels for the beds, baths, and tubs
- Five stand-up paddleboards in the garage
- A small batch of firewood waiting for you

## Built to last, lightly

The chalet is heated by geothermal heat pumps, the first geothermal retrofit in Mono County. The remodel used sustainable materials throughout, including 43% recycled Alaska Grey ceramic tile, and we pay for recycling service at the house. The project was covered in the local press as some of the greenest lodging near Yosemite. [Read the coverage](/journal/yosemite-gateway-chalet-in-the-news/).
