**Main level.** The entry hall opens to the Grand Room, with its fireplace, oak bar, and wet bar. Doors lead out to the deck and the outdoor hydrotherapy tub. Across the hall are the dining room, the kitchen, and the family room with its wood stove, plus a mud room and a half bath.

**Upper level.** The master suite with its jacuzzi and loft, the Elsie Room with its claw-foot tub, and the River Room. Each has a private bath.
