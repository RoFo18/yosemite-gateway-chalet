## The lakes

**Silver Lake** is the fly angler's lake. It drops off fast, the inlet where Rush Creek comes in holds fish all season, and the light at sunrise is reason enough to be there. Strip a woolly bugger, a Hornberg or a Grey Ghost along the drop-offs, or hang chironomids off the edge. Legend says a 9-pound, 12-ounce brook trout came out of Silver Lake in 1932. It gets bigger every year we tell it.

**June Lake** is the big, clear one. Kick a float tube along the tule beds and the inlets early, before the boats are up.

**Gull Lake** is the smallest and the most sheltered, and the closest to the chalet. When the wind is howling on the others, Gull is still fishable.

**Grant Lake** is the reservoir at the north end, open and windswept, with the best shot at a big brown on the loop in spring and fall. Fish it early; by mid-morning the wind and the water-skiers own it.

## Rush Creek

Between Silver and Grant, Rush Creek runs through meadow and willow, with a mix of wild and planted trout; that section opens the Saturday before Memorial Day and closes September 30. Below Grant Lake Dam, Rush Creek runs down to Mono Lake as a wild trout stream managed by the state, open all year, barbless artificial lures, catch and release. Small nymphs and dries in the undercuts and riffles do the work.

## Through the season

| Months | What works |
|---|---|
| Opener to June | Streamers at the drop-offs, chironomids off the edges, the inlets at first light |
| July and August | Dawn and dusk only; caddis and small dries on the creek, buggers deep on the lakes |
| September to Nov. 15 | The best of it: cooler water, browns on the move, BWOs on gray afternoons |

## Gear and a guide

Ernie's Tackle and Ski Shop in the village has gear and a report. For a day on the loop with someone who has fished it for years, call Knot at [Sierra Moments](https://www.sierramoments.com/), (760) 577-1247. He is excellent with kids, which matters when your kids are the ones holding the rod.

The chalet's five stand-up paddleboards will get you to water the shore anglers cannot reach. No fishing in the pond on the chalet grounds, though. That fish has suffered enough.

## The one thing

Timing. On the loop lakes, the first and last hour of light are worth the other ten combined.
