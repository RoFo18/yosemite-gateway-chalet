## Where to stay in June Lake

June Lake lodging comes in three kinds. Small resorts and motels near the lakes and in the village rent rooms and simple cabins, and suit couples or anglers who are out on the water all day. Condos near the June Mountain ski area put skiers close to the lifts. Private homes around the June Lake Loop give families and groups a full kitchen, separate bedrooms and a place to gather at night.

For a group that wants to be together under one roof, a whole home is the way to go. That is what the Yosemite Gateway Chalet is.

## June Lake lodging by season

**Winter, December to April.** June Mountain is a mile from the chalet and Mammoth Mountain is thirty minutes south. Tioga Road is closed, so Yosemite's east side is quiet and snowbound. Book the holidays early.

**Spring.** Corn snow, the trout opener in late April, and the first long days. Tioga Road [usually opens in late May or June](/guides/tioga-pass-opening-dates/), and the high country comes back.

**Summer.** The busiest season. Lake days on Gull, June, Silver and Grant Lakes, hikes from the door, and day trips over Tioga Pass to Tuolumne Meadows.

**Fall.** The secret. The aspens of the June Lake Loop turn gold in October, the crowds thin out, and the light is the best of the year. See our [fall colors guide](/guides/june-lake-fall-colors/).

## June Lake and Yosemite

June Lake sits on the east side of the Sierra, 27 miles from Yosemite's Tioga Pass entrance. When Tioga Road is open, it is one of the best bases for Tuolumne Meadows and the high country, with far more room than the park's own lodging. Yosemite Valley is a long day, about two to two and a half hours each way. Our guide to [lodging near Yosemite's east entrance](/yosemite-national-park-lodging/) compares June Lake with Lee Vining and Mammoth Lakes.
