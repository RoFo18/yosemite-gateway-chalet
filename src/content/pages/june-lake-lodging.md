## June Lake at a glance

June Lake is a small mountain village on the June Lake Loop in Mono County, surrounded by four lakes, the June Mountain ski area and the Ansel Adams Wilderness. It is a year-round destination: skiing and snowshoeing in winter, then hiking, climbing and trout fishing from spring through fall, when the aspens turn gold. Read our [June Lake guide](/guides/june-lake/).
