## The short answer

Most lodging inside and around Yosemite is built for couples: hotel rooms, motel rooms and tent cabins. A group that wants to cook together, gather by one fire and still sleep apart needs a whole home. The chalet is that: about 4,000 square feet, rented to one party, with three suites upstairs and a Grand Room downstairs big enough for everyone.

## How a group of eight fits

Upstairs, the **master suite** has a California King, a private two-person jacuzzi and a loft with a pull-out couch. The **Elsie Room** has a ruby-red claw-foot tub. The **River Room** is the quietest in the house, with Yost Creek below the window. Every suite has its own bath, so nobody waits in a hallway in the morning.

Downstairs, a pull-out futon in the Grand Room sleeps one or two more. The [floorplan](/the-chalet/floorplan/) shows how the two levels fit together.

## Cooking and gathering

Groups eat in. The remodeled kitchen has a six-burner stove, a double oven and a handmade copper sink, and it is stocked down to the coffee grinder and the garlic press. Dinner goes on an eleven-and-a-half-foot table of local pine. After dark, the outdoor hydrotherapy tub seats six under Carson Peak and runs all year.

## Planning a group trip to Yosemite from June Lake

From [late May or June](/guides/tioga-pass-opening-dates/) until about November, Tioga Road crosses the Sierra and the east entrance opens. A group can be at the Tioga Pass entrance in about 40 minutes and in Tuolumne Meadows in 45. Leave early: parking at popular trailheads often fills by mid-morning in summer. Yosemite Valley is two to two and a half hours away, a long but rewarding day.

Our [Tuolumne Meadows guide](/guides/tuolumne-meadows/) and [Tioga Pass guide](/guides/tioga-pass/) cover the hikes, and [lodging near Yosemite's east entrance](/yosemite-national-park-lodging/) compares June Lake with Lee Vining and Mammoth Lakes.

## Groups in every season

**Summer and fall** are for Yosemite's high country, the four lakes of the loop and five paddleboards in the garage. **Winter** is for [June Mountain](/june-mountain-lodging/), a mile away, where kids 12 and under ski free. **Spring** brings the trout opener and the first long days.

## House rules that matter for groups

**Arrival and departure.** Check-in is from 4:30 pm and check-out is at 10:00 am. Ask at least 48 hours ahead if you need to arrive earlier.

**Cars.** Three at most in the driveway. Carpool from home, or meet in Lee Vining or Mammoth and drive in together.

**The hot tub.** Guests under 18, pregnant guests, and anyone with a heart condition or pacemaker may not use it.

**Smoking and fire.** No smoking indoors. Outdoors, stay at least ten feet from the house and mind the fire danger, which is real in the Eastern Sierra.

**Pets.** None. Any animal, service or emotional support, needs advance approval with paperwork from a licensed physician; see [rates and policies](/rates-and-policies/).

**Bears.** Clear all food and trash out of your cars on arrival. Bears in the Eastern Sierra break into cars for far less than a cooler.
