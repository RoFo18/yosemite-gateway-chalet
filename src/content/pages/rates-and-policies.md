## Rates

Nightly rates run from $295 on quiet midweek nights to $995 at the Christmas peak, for up to six guests. Minimum stays change with the season.

On top of the nightly rate, every stay has:

- **A seventh and eighth guest** at $59 a night each. Infants count; the county counts them too.
- **A flat $295 cleaning fee.**
- **Mono County's 15% transient occupancy tax.**

That is the whole list. No resort fee, no linen fee, no surprise at checkout.

**For active-duty military families,** we waive the seventh and eighth guest fee. Thank you for your service. Tell us when you inquire.

## Book direct, or don't. Your call.

We will be straight with you. The booking platforms charge you a service fee, then charge us one too, and they keep tuning the system so both sides pay a little more every year. If you would like to help fund the next Airbnb billionaire or the Expedia ad budget, go right ahead. The chalet is the same chalet, and we will take great care of you either way.

If you would rather book straight through us, we would love that. [Check the availability calendar](/inquire/#availability), send us your dates, and we will send back an exact quote.

Our family has been part of the June Lake community since 1968, and the chalet is a family place, passed down and shared across generations. We are not a management company and we are not going anywhere. You will be dealing with us.

## The house rules

The June Lake Loop is small and quiet. Sound carries over water and granite, and the neighbors can hear a mouse tiptoe across cotton. These rules are why the chalet still has its permit, its neighbors and its five-star reviews. They are not negotiable.

**Guests: eight overnight, ten on the property, every person counts.** That includes infants. The limits are set by Mono County for fire safety, and our business license depends on them, so we enforce them. Over all our years of hosting, nearly every problem we have had came from parties of seven or more. Since we added the seventh and eighth guest fee, those problems have all but disappeared. Please be honest about your headcount. If it is not right, we will know.

**Cars: three, total.** Not three plus a quick drop-off, not three plus a friend who is only staying for dinner. A fourth car in the driveway, even for ten minutes, is a no.

**Pets: none.** Not in the house, not in the yard, not in the car in the driveway. Any animal, whether a service animal, an emotional support animal or anything else, must be approved before you arrive, with real paperwork from a licensed physician (MD or DO) who treats you. Certificates bought online do not count. People with real disabilities rely on these accommodations, and every fake one makes it harder for them. We take that seriously.

**Fire and snow.** If a wildfire, heavy smoke or a closed highway makes your stay impossible, we will move your dates: your payment becomes a credit toward another stay of like value within one year. We do not control fires or storms, so we do not give cash refunds for them. This is weather, not a missing side of fries.

**Events: no.** No weddings, parties or reunions. The [FAQ](/faq/) has the long answer.

**Check-in** begins at 4:30 pm. If you need to arrive earlier, let us know at least 48 hours ahead and we will do our best.

**Check-out** is at 10:00 am. A few minutes late is fine as long as housekeeping can start on time. Longer delays are charged at $50 per hour.

**Payment.** For direct bookings, a 50% deposit is due at booking, and the balance and security deposit are due one month before arrival. VRBO and Airbnb bookings follow the platform's schedule.

**Changes and cancellations.** For direct bookings, each change carries a $150 administration fee. Cancel more than 30 days before arrival for a refund of your deposit less $150. Within 30 days, no refund is given unless another party books your dates. Platform bookings follow the policy shown on the listing.

**Security deposit.** Refunded, less any charges for damage or missing items, within 14 days of departure.

**Smoking.** Not allowed indoors. Smoke outdoors at least ten feet from the house, and be mindful of fire risk. Cigarette butts left on the ground are charged at $50 each.

**Hydrotherapy tub.** The tub runs year round, through summer nights and snowstorms alike. Children under 18, pregnant guests, and people with heart conditions or pacemakers may not use the tub.

**Fishing.** No fishing in the pond on the grounds. See our [fishing guide](/fly-fishing/) for the lakes nearby.

**Snow and bears.** The roof sheds snow, so park well away from the house and approach with care. Clean all food and trash out of your car when you arrive, and leave the car unlocked: a bear that finds the door open will sniff around and leave, and one that finds it locked comes in through the window.
