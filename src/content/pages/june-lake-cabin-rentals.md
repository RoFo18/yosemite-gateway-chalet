## The short answer

Pick a resort cabin if you are two people who will be on the water all day. Pick a condo if all that matters is being near the lifts. Pick a whole home if you are a family or a group who wants separate bedrooms, a real kitchen and one place to gather at night. The Yosemite Gateway Chalet is a whole home: a 4,000 square foot log chalet on the June Lake Loop, rented to one party of up to eight.

## What to look for in a June Lake rental

**Bedrooms with their own baths.** Mornings go smoother when nobody waits for the shower.

**A kitchen you can cook in.** Restaurants on the loop are few and seasonal, so most nights you will cook.

**A hot tub.** After a day on the trail or the mountain, it is the best hour of the day.

**Winter access.** Snow can be deep from December to April. Check parking, driveway clearing and how far the cabin is from the plowed road.

**Clear fees.** Ask for the cleaning fee and the 15% Mono County lodging tax up front.

## When to book

Book as early as you can for the winter holidays, Presidents' Day weekend, the Fourth of July and October's fall color weekends. Those dates fill months ahead. Midweek stays in late spring and early summer are the easiest to find.

## June Lake by season

**Winter** brings deep snow, June Mountain a mile away and Mammoth thirty minutes south. **Spring** brings the trout opener and, when Tioga Road opens, Yosemite's high country. **Summer** is lake days, hiking and day trips to Tuolumne Meadows. **Fall** is the secret: golden aspens, empty trails and the best light of the year. ## Getting to June Lake

June Lake sits just off US 395 in Mono County, about 20 miles north of Mammoth Lakes and five hours from Los Angeles. From the Bay Area in summer, the most beautiful route crosses Yosemite on Tioga Road; in winter, when that road is closed, take US 50 or I-80 to US 395. Mammoth Yosemite Airport is about 25 minutes from the chalet, with seasonal flights. See [directions and drive times](/location/).

## A cabin near Yosemite

June Lake is one of the closest towns to Yosemite's east entrance, 27 miles from the Tioga Pass gate and about 45 minutes from Tuolumne Meadows when Tioga Road is open. For a cabin trip that includes the park, it puts you on the quiet side of Yosemite, away from the Valley crowds. See [lodging near Tuolumne Meadows](/tuolumne-meadows-lodging/).
