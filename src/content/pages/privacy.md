This site does not use advertising cookies and does not sell personal information.

When you send an inquiry, we receive the name, email address, phone number, dates, group size and message you provide. We use them only to answer your inquiry and arrange your stay. We do not sell, trade or share them with anyone except as needed to complete a booking you ask for. Form submissions are processed by our hosting provider, Netlify.

Bookings made on VRBO are governed by VRBO's own privacy policy.

To ask about or remove information you have sent us, [send a message through the inquiry form](/inquire/) or call (844) SKI-JUNE.
