## By car

June Lake is about 20 miles north of Mammoth Lakes. From US 395, take Highway 158 west into the June Lake Loop. Drive one mile past the June Mountain ski area, and the chalet is on your left at 4835 Highway 158.

- **From Los Angeles:** I-5 north to SR 14 north to US 395 north to Highway 158, about 320 miles.
- **From Orange County and San Diego:** I-15 north to US 395 north to Highway 158, about 388 miles.
- **From San Francisco and Sacramento:** in summer, Highway 120 through Yosemite over Tioga Pass, then US 395 south to Highway 158. In winter, when Tioga Road is closed, US 50 or I-80 to US 395 south, about 307 miles.
- **From Reno and Lake Tahoe:** US 395 south to Highway 158, about 155 miles.
- **From Las Vegas:** US 95 north, then west to US 395 north and Highway 158, about 323 miles.

## By air

Mammoth Yosemite Airport (MMH) has seasonal commercial service, busiest in winter, and is about 25 minutes from the chalet, with rental cars at the airport. A storm can strand a connection, so leave slack in winter plans. Reno-Tahoe International (RNO) is the nearest major airport, about three hours north.

## By bus from Yosemite

In summer, [YARTS](https://yarts.com/) runs between Yosemite Valley and the Eastern Sierra with stops in Tuolumne Meadows and June Lake. It is also the easiest way to ride into the park and hike home.

## June Lake essentials

**Fern Creek store,** about 300 yards up-canyon, is handy for tackle and groceries in a pinch. **June Lake General Store,** across from the post office in the village, has groceries, hardware, liquor and camping supplies. The **post office** is in the village and open weekdays. The **June Lake Fire Department** provides fire and emergency medical service. In an emergency, call 911.

## Bears

Clean out your car as soon as you arrive. Bears have broken into cars for a stick of gum, toothpaste or an empty soda can, and a grocery bag or cooler in view is enough to tempt them.
