Standing against the backdrop of the California Sierra, the Yosemite Gateway Chalet is a spectacular home base for adventures in June Lake, Yosemite and Mammoth country. It is a helluva joint. But then, so is my left hip.

After each day following in the footsteps of John Muir, exploring the trails of Yosemite and the hidden beauty of the woods around the chalet, my hip was aching something fierce. For every photograph I took of the fading summer, and every step around the next bend in the trail, there was a matching throb at the top of my leg.

By the end of the day it was putting a real damper on the amazing fall in June Lake, where the trees turn scarlet and the sunsets keep pace in electric orange and purple.

It was like a metronome. Click went my lens. Whump went my hip.

On the other hand: hydrotherapy.

The outdoor hot tub at the chalet is a thing of beauty. Slipping into the heat with a fine single malt in hand was like turning back the mileage on my tired body. Ten minutes of heat was like a quart of fresh oil.

Maybe a throbbing hip is a small price to pay for feeling so rich in the beauty of this place, and so lucky to experience fall in the Sierra with my wife and our friends.

Sitting in the tub as the sun dropped behind the mountains once more, I knew I would be ready to wake up and do it all again in the morning.
