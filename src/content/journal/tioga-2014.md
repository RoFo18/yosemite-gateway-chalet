Tioga Road over Tioga Pass opened on May 2, 2014, the earliest opening since 1987. The high country and its backcountry trails opened earlier than ever, with far fewer mosquitoes than most years.

For how the opening works and recent dates, see our [Tioga Pass guide](/guides/tioga-pass/).
