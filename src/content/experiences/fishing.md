Stop at Ernie's Tackle and Ski Shop in June Lake village for gear and current conditions. If you would rather not ask for directions, read on.

## June Lake

Heavily stocked, and still home to the occasional trophy fish. The harder the shoreline access, the better the fishing. Expect a range of trout, including cutthroat and brook trout.

## Gull Lake

The closest lake to the chalet, with a well-equipped marina and fish-cleaning stations. Easygoing boat fishing, plus canoes, paddleboards and kayaks for anyone who would rather paddle.

## Silver Lake

Idyllic views and phenomenal sunrises. The place for brook and rainbow trout, and some of the best fishing is in the streams that feed the lake. Legend has it that a 9 pound 12 ounce brook trout came out of Silver Lake in 1932. It gets bigger every year.

## Grant Lake

Best after 10 in the morning, once the water-skiers have to stop. Bait fishers do well where the creek pours into the lake.

## Rush Creek

One of the finest roadside fly fishing streams in the Sierra, featured in *California Fly Fisher*. Expect browns and rainbows.

## Parker Lake

Rainbow trout and a beautiful hike in one outing. See the [Parker Lake trail notes](/experiences/hiking/#parker-lake).
