## Plowed out first

{{ figure("chalet-winter-yost-creek", "The chalet in winter sun, with fresh snow on the lawn and Yost Creek running open past the pines", "The morning after a storm. Driveway plowed, creek still running.") }}

Here is how a powder morning goes at most rentals around here. The house is up a side road. The road is plowed by one guy with one plow, and he had a long night. You wait. The kids are hungry and can feel you checking your phone. By the time you get out, the June Mountain lot is nearly full, the line of cars runs an eighth of a mile, and the people who stayed somewhere better are already three runs into bluebird fresh tracks.

The chalet is right on Highway 158, and we hire professional plowing for the driveway. You are plowed out first, a mile from the lift, and in line while everyone else is still digging. That is the whole pitch. It is a good one.

## June Mountain

A mile from the chalet, [June Mountain](https://www.junemountain.com/) has 2,580 vertical feet for every level and a fraction of the crowds at its big neighbor.

### Kids 12 and under ski free

{{ figure("kids-ski-free-june-mountain", "Two young skiers in helmets and goggles riding home in a gear cart beside a pair of skis", "Done for the day, and it cost nothing: kids 12 and under ski free at June Mountain.") }}

For parents, this is the deciding number. A family can spend more on kids' lift tickets in a weekend than on the house. At June Mountain, kids 12 and under ski free all season with a no-cost child season pass, reserved on IkonPass.com or picked up at the ticket office. With the mountain a mile away, little legs can quit whenever they like and be back by the fire in ten minutes. Check [June Mountain](https://junemountain.com/explore-june-mountain/kids-ski-and-ride-free) for current terms.

### No lift lines on weekdays

{{ figure("june-mountain-chair-7-empty-runs", "Three skiers carving fresh corduroy under a deep blue sky on June Mountain, with nobody else on the run", "Off Chair 7 on a weekday. That is the whole crowd.") }}

Come Monday to Thursday and you will often ski straight onto the chair, and the groomers off Chair 7 stay empty long after first tracks.

## Ski to the hot tub

{{ figure("ski-tracks-to-the-hot-tub", "Ski tracks winding down through deep snow from June Mountain toward the chalet", "The tracks start at the top of June Mountain. They end at the hot tub.") }}

June Mountain is arguably the best lift-assisted backcountry skiing in the lower 48. From the top of the lifts, a short hike opens up a whole side of the range: big, steep, north-facing lines like the Negatives and Ghost Trees, and beyond them the Gnome Zone, San Joaquin Ridge and Carson Peak. The best of those runs funnel down into the neighborhood right behind the chalet. That is our back yard.

So yes: in a good snow year, you can ski from the top of June Mountain straight to the chalet's outdoor hydrotherapy tub. You have to be avalanche trained, carry the gear, and know exactly where you are going, because this is real avalanche terrain with no ski patrol. Which is why you should go with a guide.

{{ figure("backcountry-skier-coffee-break", "A backcountry skier in a red jacket and helmet pouring a hot drink on a high snow slope, crampons on his boots, his skis and poles planted beside his pack", "The lift does the first part. Your legs do the rest, and the coffee is not optional.") }}

**Go with Sierra Mountain Guides.** [Sierra Mountain Guides](https://sierramtnguides.com/) runs the backcountry tours at June Mountain in partnership with the resort. Its owner and lead guide, Howie Schwartz, is an IFMGA-certified mountain guide, guiding in the Sierra since 1993 and on the American Mountain Guides Association's instructor team. Howie knows the chalet. Ask him to design a day that, snow allowing, ends with your boots off and your skis leaning against the tub. Follow them on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/sierramountainguides/) for conditions, and look at their [avalanche courses](https://sierramtnguides.com/ski-tours/) if you want to learn to do it yourself.

## Rentals, tunes and gear: Ernie's

For ski and snowboard rentals, tunes, wax, and anything you forgot, go to [Ernie's Tackle and Ski Shop](https://www.erniestackleandskishop.com/) in June Lake village, 2604 Boulder Drive, (760) 648-7756. Jeremy and his crew, who will set you up on a fly rod in summer, will set your bindings in winter, and both conversations are worth having. Follow them on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/erniestacklejl/).

## Mammoth Mountain

[Mammoth Mountain](https://www.mammothmountain.com/) is about thirty minutes south. In big years it has stayed open to the Fourth of July.

## Snowshoes and ice

Rent snowshoes at Ernie's and walk from the house to the Fern and Yost Lakes trail, about a mile down-canyon. Beginners and experienced climbers find ice above Silver Lake, and some of the best ice in the West is half an hour away in Lee Vining Canyon; Sierra Mountain Guides runs courses for both.
