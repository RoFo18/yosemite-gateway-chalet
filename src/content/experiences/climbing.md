## Bouldering

June Lake is ringed by published bouldering: the Bachar Boulders, Big Springs, the Aeolian Buttes, Deadman Summit and the Ice Cream Boulders, plus a string of problems along [North Shore Drive](https://www.mountainproject.com/v/110581215) by Gull Lake, a few minutes from the chalet. Most of it is volcanic rock, featured and friction-friendly, and most of it sits at seven or eight thousand feet, so you will feel the altitude on the first warm-up and blame your shoes.

## Sunset near the tufas

The tufa towers at Mono Lake are the most photographed rocks in the county, and they are not for climbing: standing on or climbing the tufa is illegal under California State Park rules, and it breaks. The volcanic boulders and buttes around the basin are another story. At last light, with the lake going silver and the Sierra going pink behind you, a session out there is hard to beat.

## Secret spots

Some of the best rock near the chalet is not in any guidebook, and it is going to stay that way. Ask us at check-in. Bring a pad, brush off your tick marks, and do not post the location. The rock has waited a few million years for you. It can wait for the next person too.

## Ice on the Loop

In a cold winter, [Horsetail Falls](https://www.mountainproject.com/area/108571256) freezes above Silver Lake. Park near the power plant where the Loop's winter road closure begins and follow the old tramline toward the falls. The main flow runs about WI2 to 3, with North Gully, three pitches of WI2+, beside it, and there is roadside ice on the Loop as well. The falls often keep running under the ice, so quality changes from week to week. Some of the best ice in the West is half an hour away in Lee Vining Canyon.

New to ice, or new to this ice? [Sierra Mountain Guides](https://sierramtnguides.com/) runs courses and guided days on both.

## Granite in Tuolumne

When Tioga Road is open, the granite domes of [Tuolumne Meadows](/guides/tuolumne-meadows/) are forty-five minutes away: clean slabs, long moderate routes and some of the best alpine rock in the country, with none of the crowds of Yosemite Valley.

## When the weather turns

{{ figure("garage-climbing-wall-full", "The full bouldering wall in the chalet garage, covered in holds, under a stained-glass window", "The wall in the garage, for the afternoon the thunderstorm rolls in.") }}

Afternoon thunderstorms are part of summer in the high country. When one rolls in, there is a full bouldering wall in the garage. Kids love it. So do the adults who were promised a summit and got a downpour.
