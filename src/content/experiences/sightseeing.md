## Yosemite National Park

The park boundary is 5.75 miles from the chalet as the crow flies and 27 highway miles to the Tioga Pass entrance. Tuolumne Meadows is about forty-five minutes away, and Yosemite Valley, home of Half Dome and El Capitan, about two to two and a half hours when Tioga Road is open. Start with our guides to [Tioga Pass](/guides/tioga-pass/) and [Tuolumne Meadows](/guides/tuolumne-meadows/).

## Mono Lake

About seventeen minutes from the chalet. Walk among the tufa towers on the south shore at sunrise or sunset, when the limestone spires turn gold. The [Mono Lake Committee](https://www.monolake.org/) in Lee Vining is the place to learn the lake's story.

## Bodie State Historic Park

{{ figure("bodie-wagon-wheel", "A weathered wagon wheel in the grass at Bodie", None, "right") }}

A gold-mining town from the 1870s, preserved in a state of arrested decay, Bodie gives a vivid sense of California's boomtown years. About an hour's drive from the chalet. The road in is partly dirt and can close in winter. [Bodie State Historic Park](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=509).

## Devils Postpile National Monument

A wall of near-perfect basalt columns, formed when lava cooled slowly in a valley here and was later exposed by glaciers. It is reached through Mammoth Lakes and is usually open in summer and early fall, often by required shuttle. [Devils Postpile National Monument](https://www.nps.gov/depo/).

## Further afield

The Ancient Bristlecone Pine Forest, with trees more than 4,000 years old, is a long day trip south and east of Bishop.
