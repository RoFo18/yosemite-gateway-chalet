## Carson Peak Inn

Across the street from the chalet, and the best service in town. Walk over for dinner and walk home under the stars.

## Eagle's Landing

Two minutes down-canyon at the Double Eagle Resort, with mountain and waterfall views from every seat. We recommend breakfast.

## Silver Lake Resort

A summer favorite and the best breakfast on the loop. Arrive early to beat the wait.

## Whoa Nellie Deli

Hands down the best food between June Lake and the Nevada border, inside the gas station at the junction of Highways 120 and 395 just before Lee Vining. It is the last stop before Tioga Pass and the first one back. Remarkably refined, and always busy in summer.

## Tiger Bar

Soak in the local culture at a bar holding one of the oldest liquor licenses in California. Scoot down to the pool table to talk with the locals, and beware the Tiger beans.
