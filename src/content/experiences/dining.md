## 1. La Parrilla

<span class="dine-links"><a href="https://www.instagram.com/laparrilla_158/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Instagram</a></span>

The best Mexican food in the Eastern Sierra, and it sits right next to June Lake Brewing in the village. Order at the window, grab a pint next door, and eat outside with the whole loop walking by.

{{ guide_says("I have eaten tacos in a lot of places, some of them on purpose. La Parrilla is the best Mexican food on the east side of the Sierra, full stop. It is also the only place on the loop where I have reliably caught what I came for. Go after a day on the water and order like you got skunked, because you probably did.") }}

## 2. The Tiger Bar

June Lake's historic bar, now in the hands of the family behind La Parrilla, and you can taste it. Great food, a warm room, and still one of the oldest liquor licenses in California. Between the two places, the same good people are quietly making June Lake better, and we are grateful.

## 3. Eagle's Landing

<span class="dine-links"><a href="https://doubleeagle.com/eagles-landing-restaurant/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Website</a><a href="https://www.instagram.com/doubleeagleresort/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Instagram</a></span>

Two minutes down-canyon at the Double Eagle Resort, with mountain and waterfall views from every seat. We recommend breakfast.

## 4. Silver Lake Resort

<span class="dine-links"><a href="https://silverlakeresort.net/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Website</a></span>

A summer favorite and the best breakfast on the loop. Arrive early to beat the wait.

## 5. On the way to Tioga Pass

If you are driving into Yosemite over Tioga Pass, the [Whoa Nellie Deli](https://www.whoanelliedeli.com/) at the Highway 120 junction in Lee Vining is a handy stop for lunch to go in summer.

{{ note("Many restaurants here are seasonal and change their hours often. Call ahead, especially in spring and late fall.") }}
