# Fly fishing research notes (retrieved 2026-10-27)

## Regulations: 2026 California Freshwater Sport Fishing Regulations (booklet updated July 10, 2026), CCR Title 14
- General (7.00): all inland lakes open all year, 5 trout daily, 10 possession. Streams not listed in 7.50(b): last Sat. in April to Nov. 15, 5 trout; Nov. 16 to the Friday before the last Sat. in April: 0 trout, artificial lures with barbless hooks only.
- Mono Co. streams not listed: last Sat. in April to Nov. 15, no size limit, 5.
- Hot Creek (7.50(b)(61)), State hatchery property line to the Owens River confluence: all year, artificial flies with barbless hooks only, 0 trout.
- Owens River (104)(A) Upper Owens, Benton Bridge road crossing upstream to Big Springs: all year, artificial lures with barbless hooks, 0 trout. Above Big Springs see Deadman Creek (41): below Hwy 395 all year, artificial barbless, 0.
- Owens River (104)(B) Benton Bridge to Crowley Lake: last Sat. in April to July 31, 5 trout; Aug. 1 to Nov. 15, artificial lures only, 18-inch minimum, 2 trout.
- Crowley Lake (38): last Sat. in April to July 31, 5 trout; Aug. 1 to Nov. 15, artificial lures only, 18-inch minimum, 2 trout. Closed within 1,800 ft of the outlet dam.
- June, Gull, Silver, Grant lakes (Mono Co.): last Sat. in April to Nov. 15, 5 trout.
- Rush Creek (124)(A) Grant Lake Dam to Mono Lake: all year, artificial barbless, 0. (B) between Silver and Grant lakes: Sat. preceding Memorial Day to Sep. 30, 5 trout.
- Parker Lake (106): all year, artificial lures, 14-inch minimum, 2. Parker Creek (105): all year, artificial barbless, 0.
- East Walker (164), Bridgeport Dam to the Nevada line: last Sat. in April to Nov. 15, artificial lures only, 18-inch minimum, 2 trout.
- Middle Fork San Joaquin: no special listing; general stream rules (Madera Co.).
- Backcountry lakes (Fern, Agnew, Gem, Thousand Island): general lake rules unless posted.

## Facts used
- Hot Creek: "as many as 8,000 to 10,000 wild trout per mile" (California Department of Fish and Game, cited by DIY Fly Fishing). Much of the creek runs through private Hot Creek Ranch; public water below it and in the canyon.
- CDFW wild trout program management documents exist for Hot Creek, East Walker, Middle Fork San Joaquin, Rush Creek and Parker Lake.
- California golden trout: the state freshwater fish; native to Golden Trout Creek and the South Fork Kern, stocked widely into high-elevation Sierra lakes (CDFW).
- Crowley: CDFW plants about 430,000 fish a year; browns, rainbows, Lahontan cutthroat; three-midge rigs under an indicator (Kent Rianda, The Trout Fitter); damsels June to July; perch fry late July to fall; McGee Bay, Hilton Bay, Sandy Point, Layton Springs, the Owens inlet (California Fly Fisher).
- East Walker: Miracle Mile is 1.2 miles from Bridgeport Dam to the Hwy 182 bridge; average about 15 inches; records 13-lb brown and 9-lb rainbow; ideal flows 75 to 250 cfs; stop above 68°F; canyon below 182 less crowded; Nevada side has public Rosaschi Ranch water (California Fly Fisher).
- Reds Meadow Road opened June 13, 2026; since 2000 it has opened between May 22 and July 21 and closed between Sept. 7 and Nov. 5 (NPS Devils Postpile conditions page).
- Guide: Sierra Moments Fishing Adventures, Scott "Knot," Highway 158, June Lake, (760) 577-1247, sierramoments.com, facebook.com/sierramoments. 50+ years fishing the Eastern Sierra, est. 2021, insured, state licensed and bonded. Instagram: none found.
