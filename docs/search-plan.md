# Search and AI visibility plan

The goal is for this site to be the most useful, clearly stated and trustworthy source on June Lake, Tioga Pass, Tuolumne Meadows and lodging near Yosemite's east entrance, so search engines rank it and AI assistants quote it accurately. Nobody can force an assistant to recommend a business. What we control is whether machines can read the site, whether its facts are clear and consistent, and whether it is genuinely the best answer.

## What is built into the site

- Static HTML on every page, readable without JavaScript.
- One target topic per page (below), with a unique title and description.
- Answer-first guides: a direct two to three sentence answer, then detail, with tables and lists that quote cleanly.
- Structured data: VacationRental with address, coordinates, phone, occupancy, rooms, beds and amenities; FAQPage; BreadcrumbList; Article with published and checked dates; TouristAttraction for Tioga Pass, Tuolumne Meadows, June Lake and the June Lake Loop.
- robots.txt welcoming Googlebot, Bingbot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended.
- llms.txt with the property facts and key pages, and llms-full.txt with the full text of the guides.
- Every factual claim in the guides about roads, dates, trails and park rules is sourced to the National Park Service, Caltrans, Mono County or another primary source, listed with a checked date.
- Every old WordPress URL redirects permanently to its new page.
- No hidden text, keyword stuffing, doorway pages, fake reviews or instructions aimed at AI systems. Those get sites penalized.

## Off-site checklist

These carry as much weight with search engines and AI assistants as the site itself. Do them in this order.

1. **Google Business Profile.** Claim or create the listing for Yosemite Gateway Chalet. Use exactly: Yosemite Gateway Chalet, 4835 Highway 158, June Lake, CA 93529, (844) 754-5863, https://yosemitegatewaychalet.com. Category: Vacation home rental agency or Lodging. Add 20 or more photos, the description from the home page, and check-in and check-out times.
2. **Google Search Console.** Verify the domain (Netlify can serve the verification file, or add the DNS TXT record Google gives you) and submit https://yosemitegatewaychalet.com/sitemap.xml. Use the URL Inspection tool to request indexing of the home page and the six guides.
3. **Bing Webmaster Tools.** Import from Search Console and submit the sitemap. Bing feeds Copilot and several other assistants.
4. **VRBO and Airbnb.** Make the name, address, occupancy, bed list, check-in and check-out, and description match the site word for word where possible. Link to the website from the listing if the platform allows it.
5. **Reviews.** Ask every happy guest for a Google review within a week of checkout. Real, recent reviews are the strongest trust signal there is. Never write, buy or incentivize reviews.
6. **Local listings.** Get listed with Mono County Tourism (monocounty.org) and June Lake visitor resources, and with any June Lake or Eastern Sierra lodging directories you trust. Keep name, address and phone identical everywhere.
7. **Fix the old hosting.** The WordPress site was hacked with hidden spam links and backdoor files. After the new site is live, cancel the old hosting and change every password associated with it, so no copy of the hacked site stays online.
8. **Photos.** Commission new professional photography. It improves the site, the listings and the Google profile at once.

## Keep it current

- Each spring, when Tioga Road opens, add the date to the table in `src/content/guides/tioga-pass.md`, and update the `updated:` date on the Tioga Pass, Tuolumne Meadows and east entrance pages.
- Recheck park entry rules (reservations and fees) on nps.gov each spring and update the guides.
- Each October, update the fall colors guide with that year's timing.
- Once a month, ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google a few real traveler questions ("where to stay near Tioga Pass", "June Lake lodging for a group of 8", "best base for Tuolumne Meadows") and note who is named and what sources are cited. Expect changes to take 30 to 90 days to show up. Results vary run to run, so watch the direction over several months.
